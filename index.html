01 · About

Commercial strategy, backed by research and content

I bring over 9.5 years of cross-functional experience in business development, marketing, pre-sales, market research and financial research consulting. My clients and stakeholders have spanned global private equity, asset management, hedge funds, investment banking and software technology.

Most recently, as Business Development Manager at Blackbox Research in Singapore, I led APAC demand generation and brand development, pairing commercial strategy with content-driven marketing to open new opportunities and strengthen market positioning. I also use AI and digital tools to streamline workflows and make business development teams more effective.

Across my career I have built strong stakeholder relationships, developed pitch materials and thought-leadership content, and turned market insight into actions that sales teams can execute.

  • Pipeline & demand generation

    Multi-channel prospecting, cold outreach and targeted email campaigns that move prospects through discovery and qualification.

  • Market intelligence & CRM operations

    Company and executive profiling, sector mapping, clean CRM data, dashboards and documented SOPs.
